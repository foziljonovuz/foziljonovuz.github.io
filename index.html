YouTube AI Agent

YouTube AI Agent is a private tool used by one creator, Bakhrom Foziljonov, to manage his own YouTube channels: Little Pebble Learning, Prices Decoded, The Slow Planet and Eyeswap Friends.

The tool prepares videos, and after the owner approves each one in a private Telegram chat, it uploads the video to the owner's own channel through the YouTube Data API, schedules it, sets the thumbnail and adds it to the right playlist. It also reads basic statistics for these channels. It is not offered to other users.
